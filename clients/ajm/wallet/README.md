# AJ Management · Apple Wallet card

- `ajm-card.pkpass`: the card in use (2026-09-29). Built free on walletwallet.alen.ro (no account) and
  signed by WalletWallet: a black card with "AJ Management", Founder & Director · Alfred Jan Díaz, and a QR
  to `https://alfredjan.com/corporativo/?src=qr`, which plays the site's QR entry. Open it on an iPhone
  (AirDrop, Mail or Safari) to add it to Wallet.
- `build-pass.mjs`: our own version of the card (the client's logo in brand colours, all contact
  details). It needs a Pass Type ID certificate from an Apple Developer account to sign; see its header.
