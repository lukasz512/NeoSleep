## 1. Detection: why the dentist (slides 2–9)

Opening message: *"Esta semana probablemente viste pacientes con apnea obstructiva del sueño y no lo supiste. ¿Cómo el dentista puede detectarla?"* (This week you probably saw patients with obstructive sleep apnea and didn't know it. How can the dentist detect it?)

### Antecedentes médicos (slide 3)
These are the OSA-associated conditions, grouped by effect. Source: *Documento internacional de consenso sobre AOS*, doi:10.1016/j.arbres.2021.03.017.

| Group | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Enfermedades cardiovasculares | Hipertensión arterial y pulmonar, arritmias, cardiopatía isquémica, insuficiencia cardíaca, tromboembolismo pulmonar |
| Efectos metabólicos | Resistencia a la insulina, diabetes mellitus, síndrome metabólico |
| Efectos neurocognitivos | Demencia, depresión |
| Efectos neoplásicos | Cáncer |

### Cuestionario STOP-Bang (slide 5)
There are 8 yes/no items, worth 1 point each: Snoring, Tiredness, Observed apnea, Pressure (high blood pressure / under treatment), BMI > 35 kg/m², Age > 50, Neck > 40 cm, Gender male.

| Score | Risk |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Bajo riesgo: low probability of OSA |
| 3–4 | Riesgo intermedio: moderate probability |
| 5–8 | Alto riesgo: high probability |

### Clinical signs to observe (slide 7)
Source: AADSM Standards for Practice Committee. The photo collage shows:
- retrognathic profile,
- narrow/high palate,
- tooth wear (bruxism),
- fissured/scalloped tongue,
- geographic tongue,
- nasal obstruction,
- tired face / mouth breathing.

### Perfil clínico del paciente (slide 8)
- **Indicado para:** leve–moderada OSA, intolerancia a CPAP, ronquido crónico significativo.
- **Contraindicado en:** enfermedades respiratorias severas, enfermedad periodontal avanzada, apnea central del sueño.

### Roles (slide 9, AADSM Standards 2022)
1. **Odontólogo**: identifies signs and risk factors in the dental visit.
2. **Polisomnografía**: confirms the diagnosis and its severity.
3. **Neumólogo**: makes the full diagnosis and defines the treatment.

## 2. Care protocol in the dental office: 5 visits (slides 10–19)

| # | Visit | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Primera cita**: valoración y estudio Biologix | 1. NeoSleep clinical history → 2. questionnaire and initial assessment → 3. explain the sleep study → 4. hand over the Biologix sensor → 5. patient installs the Biologix app and pairs it over Bluetooth → 6. patient wears it for **3 nights** → 7. NeoSleep receives the results on the platform → 8. results go to the neumólogo → 9. neumólogo decides whether the patient is a DAM candidate. |
| 2 | **Segunda cita**: escáner bucal | 1. Patient returns the Biologix sensor → 2. scan upper and lower arch and centric occlusion → 3. record the mandibular advance with a George Gauge → 4. upload STL files and patient data → 5. confirmation by email → 6. the lab gives the DAM delivery date (**~15 days**). Patient installs **RoncoLab** and records **3 nights before** the DAM is delivered. |
| 3 | **Tercera cita**: entrega, adaptación y colocación del DAM | 1. Deliver the DAM → 2. fit and check it in the mouth → 3. explain use, insertion, removal, hygiene and care → 4. hand over the devices for each advance level (as the doctor authorizes) → 5. **3 nights** of RoncoLab recordings with the DAM. |
| 4 | **Cuarta cita**: control at **2 weeks** | 1. Adaptation and comfort → 2. ask about symptoms, sleep, snoring, tolerance → 3. review progress → 4. assess joint, muscle and occlusal discomfort → 5. if the dentist authorizes, move to the next advance level. |
| 5 | **Quinta cita**: control + next advance, **2 weeks later** | 1. Clinical evaluation: symptoms, comfort, joint/muscle/occlusal review → 2. RoncoLab results → 3. decide on the next advance (authorize it if the parameters improve) → 4. deliver the new device and instructions → 5. use it 2 weeks and keep recording. |

### Registro de mordida (slide 14)
1. Measure the advance range with the George Gauge:
   - **protrusion (MP)**: slide the jaw forward; example 6–7 mm,
   - **retrusion (MR)**: slide it back; example 0 mm.
2. Reposition the gauge at the protrusive measure and confirm there is **no joint pain** or discomfort.
3. Bite registration with putty in protrusion; let it set, then remove.
4. Intraoral scan without the gauge.
5. Intraoral scan with the gauge and bite record in the mouth.

⚠ *Siempre confirmar la comodidad del paciente y ausencia de dolor articular con la medida en protrusión.* (Always confirm the patient is comfortable and has no joint pain at the protrusive measure.)

### When to move to the next DAM (slide 16, RoncoLab)
1. The patient uses the first device for 2 weeks.
2. The patient records several nights with the RoncoLab snore meter at the end of the 2 weeks.
3. Review the results: snore index (events/h), mean intensity (dB), snoring time (% of the night).
4. If the patient is still snoring, prescribe the next device with more advance.

### Manejo de molestias articulares, musculares y oclusales (slide 17)
Steps:
1. Heat: warm compresses, 15–20 min, 2–3×/day.
2. TMJ/masseter massage: 5–10 min, 2–3×/day.
3. Analgesics and/or muscle relaxants, as indicated.
4. Keep this up for **10 days**.
5. Clinical evaluation: pain, mandibular function, muscle discomfort, occlusal relationship.
6. Decide: continue, adjust, or consider further studies.

Sources: Manfredini et al., INFORM/IADR, *Cranio* 2025, doi:10.1080/08869634.2024.2405298; APTA TMD guideline.

### New Biologix study (slide 19)
At the end of treatment:
1. New Biologix study.
2. 3 nights at home.
3. Results: apnea indices, hypoxic burden, cardiovascular risk.
4. Neumólogo reviews them.
5. Neumólogo authorizes discharge (alta).

## 3. Adaptación y seguimiento (slide 20)
Source: *Eur Respir Rev* 2023;32(168):220257.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2 weeks | Initial adaptation |
| 1 month | Symptom evaluation |
| 3 months | Clinical review |
| 6 months | Dental evaluation |
| Long term | Periodic controls |

Closing message: *"La apnea del sueño puede cambiar la vida del paciente, y el dentista puede ser quien lo detecte primero."* (Sleep apnea can change a patient's life, and the dentist can be the one who detects it first.)
